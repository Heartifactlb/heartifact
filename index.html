BEFORE YOU CREATE

A few helpful answers.

How do I order?

Choose Mini or Grand, a theme and a design. Add your details and submit the photo form to send your order request. No WhatsApp message is required.

Can I share my own idea?

Yes. Choose “Anything customized” and describe your idea. For a custom size, use “Let’s talk” to request a quote on WhatsApp.

How can I pay?

Choose cash on delivery or Whish Money. We’ll confirm Whish payment details with you directly.

How much does it cost?

Mini (small) is $25 and Grand (large) is $35, both with free delivery. For a custom size, contact us for a quote.

When will my order be ready?

Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm availability, delivery details and timing before ordering for a specific occasion.

What happens to my photos?

The photo form is provided by Tally. Your uploaded photos accompany your form submission. We use the contact details you provide to follow up about your order. WhatsApp is also available for questions. Please share only photos you have permission to use.
